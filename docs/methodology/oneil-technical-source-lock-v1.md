# O'Neil/IBD Technical Source Lock v1

No performance result was consulted.

## Entry

Breakout volume: official IBD educational material describes breakout volume at least 40% above 50-day average volume. Core operationalization uses breakout-day volume divided by the mean of the prior 50 completed sessions and confirms at ratio >= 1.40.

Buy zone: official IBD material defines the buy zone up to 5% above a proper buy point. Core preserves the 5% zone; waiting for T+1 Open is a USSY operationalization, not original O'Neil timing.

## Exit

Official IBD material documents:
- defensive loss cutting around 7-8% below purchase price;
- normal profit taking around 20-25% from the proper buy point;
- an eight-week hold exception for unusually strong rapid advances;
- weekly 10-week moving-average violation on above-normal volume as technical sell evidence.

Core v1 has no generic max-hold.
